Introduction

Traditionally, every HE subject enrolment is keyed in by admissions or student-services staff. Self-enrolment moves that routine work to the student — while the system quietly enforces every institutional rule in the background.

🕐

Less admin effort

Routine subject enrolments no longer pass through staff hands — freeing teams for the exceptions that genuinely need them.

🎓

Student autonomy

Students pick their electives and shape their study plan themselves, from any device, whenever the enrolment window is open.

🛡️

Rules always enforced

Requisites, program structure and enrolment windows are checked live. Unlike admin-led enrolment, students can never override them.

⚡

Real-time feedback

Study load, requisite warnings and enrolment deadlines are visible while the student decides — not discovered after the fact.

1Setup — before students can enrol

Everything the student experiences is controlled by the institution on Ready Student Core. Three switches matter: turning the capability on, choosing the enrolment pattern, and opening the enrolment window.

a. Turn the capability on — feature flags

Self-enrolment is enabled per provider from the Core Features page (Help → Features). The switch is "Ready Student Portals HE Student Self Enrolment" — it requires Ready Student Portals Higher Ed (and the base portal flags) to already be on. The Student Portal picks the change up automatically; nothing to deploy.

The rules engine behind the wizard is Curriculum Builder — it must also be enabled, under Development feature flags on the same page. This one is managed by superuser (level 0) accounts only, and is what powers requisite and program-structure validation during enrolment.

b. Choose the enrolment pattern

The Subject Enrolment Pattern (Administration → Preferences → Enrolment) decides which wizard students get: Yearly — one wizard covering every study period in the calendar year, or Period-based — one wizard per semester. It's a system-wide default that can be overridden per course, so different programs can run different intake models side by side.

c. Open the enrolment window

Each academic period carries its own Self-Enrolment Open and Close dates, plus a Drop Subjects Deadline — all set on the Academic Period record. These dates gate everything: before the open date or after the close date, the wizard simply isn't reachable, and the "enrolment open" banner never appears.

🗝️
The setup story in one line: flip the flag, pick the pattern, set the dates — Ready Student Core stays the single source of truth for who can enrol, in what, and when.

2Signing in — the Student Portal login

Students access the portal through a clean, branded login page. The institution's logo appears on the left — customisable per provider — with a simple username and password form on the right. A "Forgot password?" link is available for self-service recovery.

🏢

Multi-tenant

Each institution gets their own branded login page with their logo — powered by the same platform, isolated per provider.

🔑

Self-service recovery

"Forgot password?" triggers a password reset flow — no need to call the institution's help desk.

🔐

MFA supported

Multi-factor authentication can be enabled for an extra layer of security, with trusted-device support so students aren't prompted on every login.

👤
Student credentials are managed in Ready Student Core — when staff create or import a student record, a portal login is provisioned automatically. The student receives their credentials (typically via email) and can sign in immediately.

3The journey begins on the portal

The student signs in to the Ready Student Portal. When an enrolment window is open, the portal tells them — front and centre on their course page, with the closing date — so nobody misses their window.

Login→ Dashboard→ "Enrolment is open" banner→ Start Enrolment

4A guided, step-by-step wizard

The wizard lays the whole process out before anything starts: one step per study period, then a final review. The student always knows where they are and what's left.

5Requisite Rules/Validations

This is the heart of the feature. The student is free to choose — but the platform enforces the institution's rules at every step, in real time. And unlike admin-led enrolment, there is no override.

🔗

Requisite rules

Pre- and co-requisites are evaluated as the student browses, and re-checked at confirmation. Failing combinations are blocked with a clear explanation.

🏗️

Program structure

Only subjects that belong to the student's program are offered. Anything outside their curriculum simply never appears.

⚖️

Study load visibility

Credit points are totalled against the standard load for the study period as the student selects, so they can see exactly how full their semester is.

📅

Enrolment windows

Open and close dates set per academic period control access completely — outside the window, the wizard is unreachable.

💡
Worth calling out to clients: validation is done by the same curriculum engine that powers admin-led enrolment — one source of truth for rules, whether staff or students drive the process.

6Life happens — save & pick up later

Choosing subjects is a big decision. Students can save their progress at any point and come back — their selection is waiting exactly where they left it, on any device.

7Review, confirm — enrolled

Before anything is committed, the student sees a full summary of every period and every subject. One tap on Confirm runs the final validation pass and writes the enrolment into the student management system — instantly.

🔒
Safe by design: the confirmation is protected against double-submission, and if any rule fails at the last moment (e.g. a requisite), the whole submission rolls back — a student can never end up half-enrolled.

8Changed your mind? Adjust within the window

Enrolment isn't a one-shot decision. Until the institution's deadline, students can return and adjust — dropping subjects or adding new ones — through the same familiar experience.

Adjust your subjects→ Drop / add→ Review→ Subjects adjusted

9Built for every screen — mobile-first

The entire self-enrolment experience is fully adaptive. Students on phones get a purpose-built mobile layout — not a squeezed desktop view — with the same guardrails, the same wizard, and the same instant confirmation.

📱
